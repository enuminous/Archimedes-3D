    About this model

    A bounded 3D extension of Archimedes: inertial agents, source–diffusion–decay field, recursive observers, and finite lookahead.

    Buildings are solid. Station rings mark non-solid waypoints. Gravity probes collide with the floor, boundary and buildings; probes and agents do not collide with each other.

    Coherence measures state–estimate agreement. This prototype does not establish consciousness, AGI, physical EFMW validity, or real-world predictive accuracy.

    Mathematics & evidence boundary ↗

    WELCOME TO THE STATION

    Your first experiment.

    1. Run world. Watch three agents move through a 3D scalar field.
    2. Blind sensors. Compare actual and estimated positions and field readings while observations are held.
    3. Explore futures. The world pauses. Select a candidate to preview its path, then apply its first intention.
    4. Restore a checkpoint. Change the intervention to form a different branch.
    5. Drop a probe. Observe gravity and contact with the station floor.

    Orbit: drag; wheel or pinch to zoom; Shift-drag to pan. Enter world: drag to look; W/A/S/D to move; Q/E to descend/ascend. Focus the world first. Space: play/pause.

    Export a JSON session to preserve exact state, random seed and branch history. This app runs locally and sends no session data.
